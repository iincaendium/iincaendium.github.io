Art is everything. This is my moto. Every aspect of life, if means creating of at least the littlest thought — it’s art. Creation is art, and the biggest power of humans. And there is no wrong art, there is only art that we do not understand. I am creating from the pure inspiration, not putting more efforts than needed, yet, sometimes drowning in the process. I do honest poems about life. I take and edit bright pictures about something that worth being noticed. I like to play with color, since there is not much color left in modern world. We were designed to look at the sunsets and trees and sea but were look it the black and white screens, clothes, apartments. And that is sad for me. I don’t like Bauhaus, there is no originality there. No idea. So I create what wants to be created, I don’t stand in a way. And my art is something I don’t want to be estimated, it’s just a soul of mine, just like everybody else’s. Look at it, or pass by it.
