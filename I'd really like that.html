I’d really like that

Light. It just feels light.
Again, now.
After all the struggle.

After I thought I burned the switch
(I swear, I smelled the plastic stench!)
You somehow still made it toggle.

And then..
Aagh, then I started to wonder:
Am I worthy? Do I know how to do this??…

I became less scared of fog,
And my yellow-brick road
Became less hard to follow.

I imagine… Do you follow?
Writing “tory” after “his”,
I can clearly see us doing this,
Without the usual fog in the eyes.

I don’t want to talk about my feelings; I just want to be there with you.
And yes, we agreed to protect our hearts,
But then what’s the point of falling in love?
Because thought for food have tendency to expire.

I will stare at you without blinking
To learn your every little part.
You said you wish to never let me go,
And I am playing to the score in
Seeing you building house and making fire.

I’m scared, for me, it’d take a while,
But I see what this could be built for,
And, I should say, I’d really like that to become my life.

My heart rushes from the icon of an airport
On the google maps. Stupid heart. Forget it.
Nevertheless, It knows more than mind ever could
It always sees one step ahead,
And I honestly dream of treating it better.
